### Day 30 — INTERVIEW DAY 🎯
```
Morning Routine:
✅ Wake up 2 hours before interview
✅ Light breakfast
✅ Review "Tell me about yourself" once (don't over-rehearse)
✅ Take 3 deep breaths before joining the call
✅ Smile — it comes through even on phone calls
✅ Keep water nearby
✅ Have your resume open for reference
✅ Have a notepad for jotting down questions

During Interview:
✅ Listen fully before answering
✅ Take 3-5 seconds to think — silence is okay!
✅ Use STAR format for behavioral questions
✅ If you don't know something, say "I haven't worked with that,
   but here's how I would approach it..."
✅ Ask clarifying questions for coding problems
✅ Think out loud during DSA — interviewers want to see your process
```
